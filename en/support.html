Support and contact

Last updated: October 13, 2026

A bug, a purchase question, an idea?
Open a request on our support space. Tell us your device, your iOS version and what happened.

Contact support

Requests are public (a free GitHub account is required): never include personal information or a full order number.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, automatically and only on your iPhone. There is no account and no sync between devices. Deleting the game erases your progress; an iCloud or computer backup of your iPhone keeps it.

How much do my restaurants earn while the game is closed?

60% of their usual earnings, for up to 8 hours of absence. With Empire Premium: 100% for up to 12 hours. Ongoing works progress too. Changing the iPhone's clock does not earn anything.

Restore my purchases

Shop (gem icon at the top of the screen) → "Restore Purchases", with the same Apple Account. Restored: Empire Premium, the Facade Pack and chef Nonna Rosa from the Starter Pack.

Gem packs cannot be restored (Apple's rule for consumable purchases): gems are stored in your game, on your device.

A purchase did not arrive

Relaunch the game: an interrupted purchase is completed on the next launch. A purchase waiting for a parent's approval ("Ask to Buy") is credited as soon as it is approved. If the problem persists, contact support.

Get a refund

Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Change language, sound or notifications

"Goals & more" tab → Settings: language (English or French), music, sound effects, haptics, notifications and reduced motion.
